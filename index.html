Trade Register Operations

All registration, amendment, and information services managed by NTRO.

Company Registration (Înmatriculări)

Register a new legal entity — SRL, SA, SNC, SCS, RA, GIE, cooperative. Includes natural persons: PFA, Individual Enterprise (II), Family Association (IF). Full online processing via My Portal.

File mention registrations for any change to existing company data: change of directors, registered address, share capital, statutory objects, branches, or correction of material errors.

Procedures for voluntary simultaneous dissolution & liquidation, voluntary dissolution with appointed liquidator, judicial dissolution, dissolution by court decision, and automatic deregistration.

The Certificat Constatator (company status extract), certified copies of registered documents, and specialised information requests. Available online via InfoCert — instant delivery, card payment.

Insolvency Proceedings Bulletin (BPI)

Official bulletin for publishing and searching insolvency, reorganisation, and bankruptcy notices. Practitioners can publish directly online; public access is free. Integrated with the EU e-Justice insolvency portal.

Real Beneficiaries Register (RBR)

Submit, update, or consult Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) declarations as required by Law 129/2019 (AML). Declarations accepted by electronic means with e-signature, or by post. Copies and information available online.

About NTRO

Romania's Official Trade Register Authority

The National Trade Register Office (NTRO / ONRC) is a public institution subordinated to the Ministry of Justice of Romania, responsible for administering the National Trade Register — the official legal record of all commercial professionals operating in Romania.

Established under Law 26/1990 and restructured through Government Decision 902/2012, NTRO operates through its central headquarters in Bucharest (Blvd. Unirii nr. 74, Bl. J3b, Sector 3) and a network of 42 territorial offices — one in each county and one in Bucharest — all located adjacent to the competent Tribunal. Romania is fully integrated with the EU Business Registers Interconnection System (BRIS).
