About NTRO

Romania's Official Trade Register Authority

The National Trade Register Office (NTRO / ONRC) is a public institution subordinated to the Romanian Ministry of Justice. It is responsible for administering the National Trade Register — the official legal record of all commercial professionals operating in Romania.

Established under Law 26/1990 and restructured through Government Decision 902/2012, NTRO operates through its central headquarters in Bucharest and a network of 42 territorial offices, one per county plus Bucharest. It is fully integrated with the EU Business Registers Interconnection System (BRIS).

Sovereign Infrastructure
All data hosted on Romanian government servers. Full data sovereignty compliant with national law.
Secure by Design
eIDAS-compliant authentication. Qualified electronic signatures accepted throughout.
EU Interconnected
Fully integrated with BRIS and European e-Justice portal for cross-border access.
Continuously Modernising
Active digital transformation roadmap aligned with the EU Company Law Package.
